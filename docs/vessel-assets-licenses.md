# Vessel asset licenses

Everything under `artifacts/anima-protocol/public/vessels/` is built in-house. No outside avatar or 3D
service was used (no fal, Meshy, Tripo, MetaPerson, Mixamo, Ready Player Me, or Hugging Face Spaces), and
no character photo left the build machine. Nothing derived from FLAME, SMPL-X, DECA, EMOCA, MICA, MB-Lab or
Hunyuan3D is used.

The GLBs are generated by `scripts/vessel-lifelike/` (`prep_textures.py`, then
`blender -b --python build_vessel.py -- <serenity|scarlet> <out.glb>`). They use Blender 4.2.3 headless and the
MPFB2 2.0.18 add-on. The scripts expect the tool and asset checkout under `/workspace/lifelike-vessel/tools`
(edit `T`/`O` in `prep_textures.py` and `TOOLS`/`TEX` in `build_vessel.py` to move them).

## Tools (not redistributed; their licenses do not reach the outputs)

| Tool | License | Note |
|---|---|---|

- The sashes (waist band and tail, diagonal band and tail), the ruby pendant and earrings.
